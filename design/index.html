Design system · v1.0 · July 2026

Sela

The visual system for Sela — the connective layer for pharmacy in Iraq. Arabic-first, RTL-native, built to stay coherent as the product grows from a community feed into an operating system.

01

Logo

Two interlocking links form the letter S and the idea of صلة — a connection. A pulse line runs through the joint. The mark carries the meaning; nothing else in the system needs to.

Primary lockup

Mark only — app icon, avatar, favicon

Below 32px Drop the pulse line. It fills in and turns the mark into a smudge. Links only.

Rules

Do
  • Clear space on all sides equal to the height of the mark.
  • Minimum lockup width 96px, minimum mark 20px.
  • Arabic صلة always sits above Latin Sela, never beside it.
  • On photography, use the solid teal or dark version only.
Don't
  • Don't recolor the mark outside teal-500, white, or ink-800.
  • Don't add a shadow, glow, outline, or gradient to the mark.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or reweight the strokes.
  • Don't set Sela in any face other than Plus Jakarta Sans 700.
Note: this is a code-ready rebuild of the mark for use in HTML and SVG. Keep the original vector file as the master for print and large-format work.

Usage

RuleDetail
One accent per screenTeal is the only color allowed on primary actions. A domain color appears only on content belonging to that domain — a recall card, a job badge. Never as a button.
Tint, don't fillDomain colors appear as the 50 tint background with the 700 text on top. The 500 is for icons, dots, and 2px accents only.
No gradientsEvery surface is a flat fill. No mesh, no glow halo, no soft radial wash behind headings. This is the single biggest change from the current mockups.
Contrast floorBody text ink-800 on white is 13.9:1. Never place teal-500 text on white for body copy — it fails at 2.6:1. Use teal-700 for links.

Typography

Two families, not three. Tajawal carries Arabic, Plus Jakarta Sans carries Latin, IBM Plex Mono carries data. Cairo is retired — running two Arabic faces makes the system drift within a month.

Language rules

Drug namesAlways Latin script, never transliterated. Pantoprazole, not بانتوبرازول. Pharmacists read them in Latin and search them in Latin.
NumeralsWestern digits (0–9) everywhere, including inside Arabic text. Prices, doses, counts and dates stay scannable and sortable.
Arabic registerModern Standard for anything official or clinical. Iraqi dialect is allowed in community copy and marketing, never in a warning or a dosage note.
Sentence caseEnglish UI is sentence case. Never Title Case, never ALL CAPS except the mono eyebrow labels.
Line lengthArabic reads slower at width — cap body text at 60 characters, not 75.

06

RTL

Arabic is the primary language of this product, not a translation of it. Build RTL-first and let LTR be the mirror, not the other way around.

Logical propertiesUse margin-inline-start, padding-inline-end, inset-inline. Never margin-left. One dir attribute then flips the whole product correctly.
What mirrorsLayout, navigation, sidebars, progress bars, chevrons, back arrows, alignment.
What never mirrorsThe Sela mark. Drug names. Numbers. Phone numbers. Clocks. Charts with a time axis. Play buttons.
Mixed stringsWrap Latin drug names inside Arabic sentences in <bdi>. Without it, punctuation jumps to the wrong end and the sentence looks broken.
Line heightArabic needs more. Use 1.75 for Arabic body against 1.65 for Latin.

07

Motion & voice

Durations120ms hover · 180ms panel · 240ms modal · 320ms page
Easingcubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1) — everything
Never animatePrices, doses, stock counts, or anything a pharmacist reads to make a decision. It reads as unstable data.
Reduced motionRespect prefers-reduced-motion. Opacity only, no transform.
Voice — do
  • Say what happens: "Find medicine", not "Get started".
  • Errors state the problem and the fix, in one line.
  • Empty states invite an action, not apologize.
  • Same word for the same action everywhere.
Voice — don't
  • No "seamless", "empower", "revolutionize", "unlock".
  • No exclamation marks in system messages.
  • No first person. The product isn't a friend saying "I".
  • No clinical advice phrased as a recommendation.
08

What changed from the mockups

Seven decisions that turn the current designs into a system. The logo, the teal, and the product naming are unchanged.

#ChangeWhy
1All gradients and glow halos removedThey date the product to 2021 healthtech and cost render performance on the low-end Android devices most of your users hold.
2Emoji removed from the interface👋 and 🔥 in a product handling drug recalls undercuts the trust the brand is selling.
3Cairo retired, Tajawal onlyTwo Arabic families with no assigned roles guarantees inconsistency within weeks.
4Domain colors given fixed meaningSix colors used decoratively is noise. Six colors used semantically is a navigation aid.
5One primary button per screenThe current dashboard has four teal calls to action competing. None of them wins.
6Growth percentages pulled off metric cards"+12.5% from last month" on a live product with no users is a credibility risk in a demo.
7Arabic set as the build defaultThe mockups are English-first with Arabic screens attached. Your market is the reverse.
